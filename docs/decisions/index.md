# Decisions

Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) capture the *why* behind significant design choices. Each ADR is a short, dated document that explains the context, options, decision, and consequences.

## Conventions

Each ADR follows this structure:

1. **Title** — a short, descriptive name.
2. **Status** — *Proposed*, *Accepted*, *Superseded*, or *Deprecated*.
3. **Date** — when the decision was made.
4. **Drivers** — who proposed or championed the decision.
5. **Context** — the problem and forces in play.
6. **Options Considered** — alternatives and their trade-offs.
7. **Decision** — what was chosen.
8. **Consequences** — what becomes easier and what becomes harder.

When an ADR is superseded, the new ADR is linked from the *Supersedes* field of the old one.
